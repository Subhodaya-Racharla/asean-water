Who We Are

Quality Water,
Trusted by Families

Asean is a premium packaged drinking water brand from the house of Vijay Chakravarthy Enterprises, based in Ankampalem, Atreyapuram Mandal, Ambedkar Konaseema District, Andhra Pradesh.

We operate our own state-of-the-art manufacturing and bottling unit where every drop goes through rigorous multi-stage purification — ensuring it meets the highest food-safety standards before it reaches your table.

🏭
In-House Manufacturing Full control over production, bottling & label printing at our own facility.
🔬
Strict Quality Testing Multi-stage filtration and regular quality checks to guarantee purity.
📋
Certified & Compliant Operating under food safety guidelines. Certifications — FSSAI registration applied.
